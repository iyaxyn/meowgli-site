Meowgli Digger

A game for teenagers about a resourceful kitten and traps for troublesome pests.

In development

  1. Choose a spot and dig a pit.
  2. Lure a pest into your trap.
  3. Pack down the earth, collect what it drops and move on.

Short levels, ladders and pests with different habits. Meowgli needs quick paws and a good plan to catch them.
