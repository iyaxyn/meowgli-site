Meowgli Dig: Burrow Bandits Support

Write to us: hello@meowgli.app. We read every message. Please include your device model, iOS version and what exactly happened: it helps us reply faster.

How to restore a purchase

Purchases go through the App Store. If you bought something earlier or installed the game on another device, sign in with the same Apple Account, open the purchase screen, tap "Restore" and wait a few seconds. The purchase is checked with the App Store, so you need an internet connection.

Moving to a new device

The purchase belongs to your Apple Account, not to the device: on the new iPhone or iPad, sign in with the same Apple Account, open the purchase screen and tap "Restore". Progress (levels, stars, coins, outfits) is stored on the device and goes into your iCloud or computer backup: if you set up the new device from a backup, your progress comes back with the game. If you install the game from scratch, progress starts over, and the purchase is restored.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds, not us: https://reportaproblem.apple.com Pick the game in your purchase list and the reason for the refund. Apple makes the decision.

Resetting progress

To start over, delete the game from your iPhone or iPad and install it again: the game's data is removed with it. Your purchase is not lost: restore it with the "Restore" button on the purchase screen. If you want to keep your progress, back up the device before deleting.

The game will not start or crashes

Update the game and iOS to the latest versions and restart the device. If that does not help, write to us: hello@meowgli.app. The game is made for iPhone and iPad and is played in landscape.

Privacy

The game does not collect data about players. Details are in the privacy policy: https://meowgli.app/en/digger/privacy/
