Meowgli Dig: Burrow Bandits Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026.

Meowgli Dig: Burrow Bandits is a puzzle-action game for iPhone and iPad, made by Stanislav Arnaut ("we"). This page explains what data the game keeps, where it is stored and who can see it.

In short: we do not collect data about players. The game has no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and we have no server the game could send anything to.

What stays on the device

So that you can continue where you stopped, the game stores on the iPhone or iPad itself:

  • progress: completed levels, stars, coins, items and outfits you own;
  • settings: language, sound and music, difficulty, control scheme and button layout;
  • purchase status as reported by the App Store;
  • small technical notes, such as when the game last asked iOS to show Apple's rating window, and a local play log used only on the device.

This data never leaves the device, and we never receive it. It is deleted together with the game. If the device is backed up to iCloud or to a computer, the game's data goes into that backup like any other app's data; the device owner controls this.

Purchases

Purchases go through the App Store, and the game receives no payment details. Apple handles the payment: we never see your name, Apple Account or card details. The game learns from the App Store only whether the purchase is active. You can restore a purchase with the "Restore" button on the purchase screen. Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Ratings

After a level, the game may ask iOS to show Apple's standard rating window, at most three times a year. Whether it appears is decided by iOS. Ratings and reviews go to Apple; we see only what everyone sees on the App Store. The "Rate" button opens the game's review page in the App Store.

Emails and links

"Support", "Privacy" and "Our projects" open pages on meowgli.app in your browser. "Write to the author" opens your own Mail app; an email is sent only if you tap Send. We then receive the address, the text and anything you add. We use it to reply and to improve the game, and never pass it on. We keep the conversation for no longer than 12 months after the last email and delete it sooner on request.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports. They contain no name, email address or Apple Account. We use them to find and fix bugs.

What the game does not have

  • ads or advertising identifiers;
  • third-party analytics or tracking;
  • accounts, sign-up, chat or user-generated content.

We do not sell or share data with third parties, because we have none.

Age

The game is intended for players aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

If you have written to us, you can ask what data of yours we have, get a copy, correct or delete it, or object to its use: write to hello@meowgli.app. We process your email because you contacted us and expect an answer (for EU residents: legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Residents of the EU and the UK may complain to their national data protection authority. Data on the device is yours to delete, together with the game.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the game ever starts collecting data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details first.
