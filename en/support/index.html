Meowgli Books Support

Why is Our Farm sometimes free and sometimes locked?

In version 1.1, Our Farm is free every other week. In the other weeks, it is available with a subscription; you can also keep it by unlocking it with apples earned through reading. Your bookmark is saved.

What are apples for?

Children earn apples through reading. They can use them to unlock books marked with an apple price on the shelf and to keep books of the week. The app shows how many apples are needed before a book is unlocked.

Subscription and trial period

A grown-up subscribes, and Apple handles payment. Check the available options, price and trial terms in the App Store before confirming. A trial is not necessarily available to every account: follow the offer Apple shows you.

How to cancel

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Meowgli Books → Cancel Subscription. The subscription stays active until the end of the paid period.

How do I restore purchases?

From the shelf, open “Grown-ups” → “Restore purchases”. This restores access to purchases, not bookmarks or reading progress.

Family Sharing

If a grown-up has turned on Family Sharing, the subscription works for the whole family — up to six people.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

Recorded narration and reading recognition

Recorded narration of the books is available in Russian (ru), English (en), German (de) and Spanish (es). Reading recognition is a separate feature: it runs on the device if the device supports on-device recognition of the book’s language, the feature is enabled, and microphone and speech recognition permissions have been granted. A translation of a book or this policy does not guarantee that recognition works in that language.

The microphone is used only for reading out loud: your child reads a page and the app marks the words already read. While a book page is open, the app listens by itself, with no button to press; you can turn this off in “Grown-ups” → “Settings” → “Reading out loud”. Speech is recognized by the iPhone or iPad itself, with no internet connection. No sound is recorded, no recognized text is kept, and neither the sound nor the recognized text is sent to us or to Apple. Without microphone access the app still works, just without marking the words. You can turn access off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition.

On iOS 26 or later, you can tap “Download” in the app’s settings to install a missing speech recognition language pack. Downloading requires an internet connection: a system service downloads the pack from Apple’s servers. After installation, iOS manages the pack and may update it. Reading recognition still runs on the device; the app does not send a voice recording or recognized text to download the pack.

Can we read without internet access?

Books that have already been downloaded and are available to you can be read offline. Downloading a missing speech recognition language pack requires internet access; this is a separate download, not a requirement for reading the text.

Your child's progress

Bookmarks, stickers and progress are kept only on this device and are deleted together with the app.

How do I contact support?

Write to hello@meowgli.app. A grown-up sends the message; we use it only to reply.
