Subscription

A grown-up subscribes in the app after the grown-up check. The price and the free trial are shown before you buy. Apple handles the payment.

How to cancel

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Meowgli Books → Cancel Subscription. The subscription stays active until the end of the paid period.

Subscription on a new device

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the subscription screen in the app and tap “Restore purchases”.

Family Sharing

If a grown-up has turned on Family Sharing, the subscription works for the whole family — up to six people.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds: https://reportaproblem.apple.com

Reading out loud and the microphone

The microphone lets the app mark the words your child has read. Speech is recognized on the iPhone or iPad itself: no sound is recorded or sent anywhere. To turn it off: For grown-ups → Settings → Reading out loud.

Your child's progress

Bookmarks, stickers and progress are kept only on this device and are deleted together with the app.
